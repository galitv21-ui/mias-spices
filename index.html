In the kitchen

Easy ways to use them

5 min

Dukkah avocado toast

Mash avocado on toasted sourdough, add a squeeze of lemon and a generous sprinkle of Dukkah.

30 min

Sheet-pan shawarma chicken

Toss chicken thighs and sliced onion with olive oil and 2 tbsp Shawarma blend. Roast at 220 °C until crisp. Serve in pita.

40 min

Red Gold potatoes

Cut potatoes into wedges, toss with oil and 1 tbsp Red Gold Blend, and roast at 200 °C until golden.

On the grill

Mediterranean BBQ wings

Rub wings with oil and Mediterranean BBQ blend, rest 30 minutes, then grill or air-fry until sticky and charred.
