Our story

It started with a grandmother's tin.

Mia grew up in a kitchen where nothing came from a supermarket packet. Spices were bought whole, toasted in a dry pan and ground just before cooking, and the whole house smelled of it.

Mia's Spices is an attempt to bottle that. We buy whole spices in small quantities, grind them in small batches, and mix every blend by hand. If a batch doesn't smell right, it doesn't get packed.

— Mia

In the kitchen

Three easy ways to start

5 min

Za'atar flatbread

Mix 2 tbsp za'atar with 4 tbsp olive oil. Spread over pita or flatbread and bake at 220 °C for 6 minutes.

10 min

Sumac onions

Thinly slice a red onion, toss with 1 tsp sumac, a pinch of salt and lemon juice. Pile onto anything grilled.

25 min

Baharat chickpeas

Toss drained chickpeas with oil, 1 tbsp baharat and salt. Roast at 200 °C until crisp. Snack or salad topper.
